THE BASICS

Understanding Climate Change

What Is Climate Change?

Climate change is a widespread and long-term disruption of the Earth's climate, particularly in temperature and precipitation patterns, leading to changes in the composition of the atmosphere due to both human and natural causes.

🌍

WHY DOES IT HAPPEN?

Causes of Climate Change

Climate change happens mainly because of human activities that increase greenhouse gases in the atmosphere.

Burning Fossil Fuels

Burning coal, oil, and natural gas in factories and power stations releases carbon dioxide into the atmosphere.

Deforestation

Cutting down trees reduces the Earth's ability to absorb carbon dioxide.

Transportation

Cars, buses, trucks, and airplanes release greenhouse gases when they burn fuel.

Agriculture and Livestock

Farm animals produce methane, and some farming activities release greenhouse gases.

Waste and Pollution

Decomposing waste in landfills produces methane, which contributes to global warming.

Industrial Activities

Factories and industrial processes release greenhouse gases into the atmosphere.

THE CONSEQUENCES

Impacts of Climate Change

Climate change has many negative effects on human health and the environment. It affects people's lives, animals, plants, and natural resources.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Impacts on Humans

🌡️

Heat-Related Illnesses

High temperatures can cause heat exhaustion, dehydration, and heatstroke.

🌫️

Respiratory Diseases

Air pollution and dust can increase breathing problems, asthma, and lung diseases.

🦟

Infectious Diseases

Rising temperatures can help mosquitoes and other disease-carrying insects spread.

💧

Food and Water Shortages

Droughts and floods can reduce access to safe drinking water and food.

🌳 Impacts on the Environment

🧊

Melting Ice

Rising temperatures cause glaciers and ice sheets to melt.

🌊

Rising Sea Levels

Rising sea levels threaten coastal cities and agricultural lands.

🏜️

Drought and Desertification

Climate change can increase water shortages and make some areas drier.

🔥

Forest Fires

Hotter and drier conditions can increase the risk of wildfires.

🐾

Loss of Biodiversity

Many animals and plants lose their natural habitats and may become endangered.

⛈️

Extreme Weather Events

Climate change can increase the intensity or likelihood of some heatwaves and heavy rainfall events.

Protecting Our Planet Starts With Understanding

Learning about climate change helps us make better decisions and take action for a sustainable future.
