OUR APPROACH

Ideas inspired by real environmental challenges

These are proposed educational prototypes. Each one demonstrates a concept that could be tested and improved before any real-world use. The projects are not presented as certified products or proven solutions.

7 ideas
01
Cooling & Air

Cool Air Wall

A small wall model that explores how porous materials can support airflow and reduce heat buildup.

Model: Build two small wall samples, one solid and one made with a porous material.

Test: Compare airflow and temperature under the same conditions.

Challenge: High indoor temperatures and reliance on mechanical cooling.

02
Cooling & Air

Sun Shield Window

An adjustable reflective cover designed to limit direct sunlight while allowing natural light into a room.

Materials: A small window model, reflective material and an adjustable opening mechanism.

Test: Compare temperatures in two similar model rooms.

Challenge: Indoor heat and excessive use of air conditioning.

03
Water & Energy

Smart Sunlight Irrigation

A model that checks soil moisture and supplies water when the plant needs it.

Materials: Soil moisture sensor, small pump, tubing and controller.

Process: The sensor measures soil moisture and the controller activates the pump when needed.

Test: Compare the amount of water used with traditional timed irrigation.

Challenge: Avoidable water waste in irrigation.

04
Clean Energy

Solar Street Light

A miniature street light that uses a solar panel to power an LED.

Materials: Small solar panel, LED and a suitable charge-control circuit.

Process: The panel converts sunlight into electrical energy.

Test: Observe lighting time under different charging conditions.

Challenge: Street lighting energy consumption.

05
Water & Energy

Heat to Plant Water

A solar still model that uses the sun's heat to evaporate water and collect condensed water.

Model: A small solar distillation model with a transparent cover.

Process: Sunlight warms the water. Vapour condenses on the cover and runs into a collection point.

Test: Measure the collected amount over a fixed period.

Challenge: Water scarcity and water treatment.

Water from this educational model must not be treated as drinking water.

06
Cleaner Air

Kitchen Smoke to Cleaner Air

A demonstration model showing how airflow and filter layers may capture some airborne particles.

Model: A small enclosure, low-voltage fan and replaceable filter layers.

Explore: Study how air moves through the model and how filter materials differ.

Challenge: Indoor air quality and particles produced during cooking.

This is an educational prototype, not a certified air purifier.

07
Green Cities

Green Bus Stop

A public transport shelter concept combining shade, plants, solar lighting and rainwater collection.

Design: A shaded roof, plants, solar lighting and a rainwater collection concept.

Explore: Create a scale model and explain how each feature could improve the waiting area.

Challenge: Heat exposure in public spaces and sustainable urban development.

FROM MODEL TO IMPACT

Test it. Measure it. Improve it.

A strong environmental idea needs evidence. For every prototype, our next step is to define a fair test, record results and improve the design based on what we learn.
