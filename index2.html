ABOUT EGYPT

Egypt and Climate Action

Building a Sustainable Future

Egypt is working to address climate change through environmental protection, renewable energy, water conservation, sustainable agriculture and green development.

These efforts aim to protect natural resources, reduce environmental pollution and improve the quality of life for future generations.

Each governorate has different environmental needs depending on its geography, natural resources and economic activities.
